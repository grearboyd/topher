Why Topher

Topher is a friend

Not necessarily a literal person — though he is, we've worked with him for two years — but that's the brand character. Topher is the friend who's always a good hang. You might not see him for two years, but when you do, nothing has changed.

He's outdoorsy, but not performative about it. He likes camping, fishing, hiking, road trips, cabins, campfires, and being on the water. He doesn't need to climb Everest. To be cheesy about it: Topher isn't about the adventure, it's about the friends made along the way.
